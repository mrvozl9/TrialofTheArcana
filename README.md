1. GAME OVERVIEW

	Game Title: Trial of the Arcana

	Genre:
	* 2D Metroidvania
	* Dark Fantasy Adventure
	* Educational Platformer

	Platform: PC

	Target Audience: Players who enjoy...
		* Metroidvania exploration,
		* Dark fantasy stories,
		* Challenging gameplay,
		* Tarot symbolism and lore.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

	Game Introduction:

		* Trial of the Arcana is an educational 2D dark fantasy Metroidvania, 	
	where players explore a cursed world and unlock Tarot-based abilities by collecting Major Arcana Cards.
		* Each Arcana Card represents both:
	   	   -A gameplay ability,
	    	   -And a symbolic Tarot meaning.
		* Players learn the meanings of the Tarot through exploration, combat, puzzles, NPC dialogue, and progression.
		* Magical cat spirits are scattered throughout the map and act as guide NPCs. These cats provide players with 
	information about the Tarot cards, the Dark Realm, hidden lore, and gameplay mechanics. Each cat helps the player better 
	understand the symbolic meaning of the Arcana they discover.
		* The game focuses on movement, exploration, symbolic storytelling, and ability-based progression.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

	High Concept:

		* The player travels through the Dark Realm, a world filled with cursed souls and symbolic trials.
		* As players collect Arcana Cards, they unlock new abilities inspired by the meanings of the Tarot cards.
		* Examples:
		   -The Fool = beginnings and freedom → unlocks movement abilities.
		   -The Magician = power and mastery → unlocks magic abilities.
		   -The High Priestess = hidden knowledge → reveals secrets and hidden paths.
		* The player must survive dangerous environments, defeat enemies, and learn the power of the Arcana to escape the Dark Realm.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

	Core Gameplay Loop:

		1. Explore the world,
		2. Fight enemies and avoid hazards,
		3. Complete quests,
		4. Collect Arcana Cards,
		5. Unlock new abilities,
		6. Access new areas,
		7. Learn more about the world and Tarot symbolism.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

	Win & Loss Conditions:
		
		* Loss Condition:
			The player's health drops to zero from enemy attacks or environmental hazards, resulting in a "Game Over" screen and respawning 
	at the last save point.

		* Win Condition:
			The player successfully collects the required Arcana cards, masters their abilities to defeat the final trial, and escapes the 
	Dark Realm, triggering the "Victory/End" screen.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

	Main Features:

		* Arcana-based ability progression,
		* Educational Tarot symbolism,
		* Exploration-focused Metroidvania gameplay,
		* Dark fantasy world design,
		* NPC quests and lore,
		* Ability-gated progression,
		* Challenging platforming and combat.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

	Main Map Zones:

		1. Ghostly Cemetery
		2. Cursed Forest
		3. Magician’s Tower
		4. Lava Fissures
		5. Snake’s Lake
		6. Yellow Woods

------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

	Player Mechanics:

 		1. Movement:
		   * Move Left / Right → Arrow Keys
		   * Jump → Z
		   * Float → Hold Z
		   * Dash → C

		2. Combat:
		   * Air Slash → X
		   * Earth Shield → S
		   * Fire Meteor → D
		   * Holy Water → F

 		3. UI Interactions:
		   * Open Book UI → Tab
		   * Pause Menu → Esc

3. EDUCATIONAL CONCEPT

	Pedagogical Goal:

		The primary educational goal of Trial of the Arcana is to teach players the symbolic meanings, 
	themes, and interpretations of the Major Arcana Tarot cards.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

	Applied Learning Theory: Constructivism

		The game utilizes Constructivist learning theory, where players actively build their understanding 
	of Tarot symbolism through experiential learning and exploration.Rather than simply reading definitions, 
	players construct meaning by actively applying the cards' concepts to overcome in-game obstacles and navigate the world.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

	How Game Mechanics Facilitate Learning:

		The ability-gated Metroidvania progression system directly links gameplay mechanics to educational outcomes. 
	For example, when a player collects "The Fool" card, they unlock new movement abilities (like Dash or Float).This physical 
	game mechanic teaches the player that "The Fool" represents freedom, new beginnings, and taking a leap of faith. 
		Similarly, "The Magician" unlocks magical combat abilities (mastery and power), while "The High Priestess" reveals 
	hidden paths (intuition and hidden knowledge). By requiring players to use these specific abilities to progress, they internalize 
	the psychological and symbolic meaning of the cards through direct, repetitive gameplay action.
